ABOUT

Be Zen Studio is an open-air shala in the jungle of Srithanu, five minutes from the sea. We opened with one simple idea: a calm, friendly place where anyone can move, breathe and slow down.

No mirrors, no pressure. Just kind teachers, warm light and the sound of the trees.
